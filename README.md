# Anatomy of a Breakout Season: The 2026 Golden State Valkyries

## The question
Why did the Golden State Valkyries go from 23 wins in 2025 to 32 wins in 2026,
and what does it mean for 2027?

## Hypotheses
Written before looking at any data:
1. They improved mainly on defense.
2. They take and make more threes than last year.
3. Returning players played a bigger share of the minutes.
4. The team's results depend heavily on a few key players.

## What I'm building
- **Layer 1, what changed:** offense, defense, threes and roster continuity, 2025 vs. 2026, compared against the rest of the league
- **Layer 2, who drove it:** player-level analysis (trends, minutes vs. production, and on/off impact if the data allows)
- **Layer 3, what's next:** a model trained on WNBA history to forecast 2027 wins as a range
- **An interactive Tableau dashboard** with a player dropdown

## Data
- WNBA player and team box scores, plus play-by-play where available (via the `wehoop` R package)
- Basketball Reference as a backup source

## Tools
R (data pulling), Python (cleaning, analysis, forecast), Tableau (dashboard)
